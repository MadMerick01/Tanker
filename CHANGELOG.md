# What's changed

Every change to Tanker, in plain English, newest first. Each build is a new download on the [Releases](../../releases/latest) page.

## Coming next

Changes that are finished but not in a download yet. They move into a numbered build as soon as it's published.

## Build 35 · 8 Nov 2026

- No changes in the game. The notes and changelog now name the author as MadMerick.

## Build 34 · 6 Nov 2026

- **New Aim up 1° and Aim down 1° buttons for the VR revolver.** On the Rig tab, next to the VR revolver, the HUD now shows how far the gun is tilted from your controller (for example -10°, negative is down) with two buttons that tilt it up or down one degree. Use them with the red laser on to line the gun up with BeamNG's black pointer line, instead of typing the setting into the console. The VR revolver remembers the tilt between sessions. The buttons only appear when the BeamNG VR Controller Poses mod is installed and new enough to have the aim setting.
- Your save still works, and there's nothing to do except download the new tanker.zip.

## Build 33 · 6 Nov 2026

- **Hoons are better at getting through the scrub.** A Hoon now looks ahead along both edges of its car as well, so it no longer tries to squeeze through a gap between two bushes that's narrower than itself. When a bush has others beside it, the Hoon goes round the open side of the whole clump. Once it picks a side it sticks to it for a moment instead of weaving left and right.
- **Hoons now see bushes on hills.** Their view ahead follows the ground, so a Hoon spots a bush up a slope or just over a crest in time instead of running into it. The bottom of a steep hill still isn't mistaken for a bush.
- Your save still works, and there's nothing to do except download the new tanker.zip.

## Build 32 · 6 Nov 2026

- **Hoons now steer round solid bushes, rocks and trees.** In the desert a Hoon looks ahead for anything solid in its path and swerves round it, easing off if it's right in front. Hillsides don't count. A Hoon going for a sideswipe still holds its line.
- **Hoons and tankers no longer get stuck on bushes for good.** If a Hoon or the TankerMob is caught on something for a few seconds while it's behind your camera and at least 50 m away, it's moved a few metres on past it. You never see it happen, and any damage it has stays.
- Your save still works, and there's nothing to do except download the new tanker.zip.

## Build 31 · 6 Nov 2026

- **The rogue AI now watches the roads, and sends drones.** Stay on a road for more than 30 seconds and a drone hits you: you hear it coming for the last 5 seconds, then an explosion beside your truck damages it and may burst a tyre. A banner at the top of the screen counts down once you've been on a road for 5 seconds, so crossing a road is safe. Stay on the road and another drone comes 20 seconds later. Get off the road before it arrives and it misses. Stay off the road for 5 seconds and the AI loses you, and the count starts again. Tanker is meant to be played off road, so build a truck that can handle it and navigate by compass.
- **Hoons and tankers no longer appear where you can see them.** A new Hoon or tanker is now always put somewhere in the half of the world behind your camera, so nothing pops into view in front of you. If you look back over your shoulder, it can come from up ahead instead. This covers the Hoons from Spawn a Hoon, the Hoon that comes up the road every few km and the tanker from the spawn button.
- Your save still works, and there's nothing to do except download the new tanker.zip.

## Build 30 · 6 Nov 2026

- **Hoons now cut you off when you turn.** In the desert, a Hoon reads how fast you're turning and heads for where you'll be by the time it gets there. It cuts across the inside of your turn instead of chasing round the outside, so a hard turn no longer shakes them off.
- **Hoons now go for your back wheel to spin you out.** A Hoon behind you at one of your back corners may line up just off that corner instead of swiping. It then drives its nose through your back wheel, a little faster than you and without braking, to shove your back end round. You're told which back wheel it's lining up on. The Hoon list on the Road tab says when one is going for your back wheel.
- **Hoons respect your revolver.** While it's drawn, Hoons getting into place weave from side to side so they're harder to hit. Hoons at your back corners stay there, out of your line of fire, and more of their attacks are the back-wheel kind. A Hoon you hit but don't stop backs off. It drops to a far spot, keeps further out and doesn't attack for 6 seconds. Four hits still stop a Hoon.
- These changes are for the desert. On roads, Hoons drive as before. Your save still works.

## Build 29 · 6 Nov 2026

- **Hoons in the desert no longer touch the brakes.** When a Hoon changes spot around you, lines up or swipes you, it never brakes. When it needs to slow down, it lifts off the throttle and asks for no more than a little under your speed. So it moves round you sideways or forwards and no longer drops back behind you. Hoons still brake when they box you in, or when they pull up to rob you.
- From inside your truck beside a car, the siphon window and the Road tab now say "Get out and connect the pump to siphon fuel to your tanks."
- **A Hoon's sideswipe now matches your speed and turns into you suddenly**, without braking or slowing for the turn, instead of speeding up into you.
- Your save still works.

## Build 28 · 6 Nov 2026

- **Siphoning now happens in a window in the middle of the screen, and you siphon on foot.** Park within 15 m of a car, get out and walk up to it, and the window offers to siphon it. From inside your truck, the window and the Road tab tell you that you must be on foot. Your truck must stay parked within 15 m of you, since the fuel goes into it. Hoons nearby don't stop you siphoning, though the window warns you they're about. You can't save while you're on foot. If your truck has a bed tank, you choose where the fuel goes: **Into your truck** or **Into the bed tank**. While the fuel flows, the window shows both tanks and a **Stop** button, then what you took for a few seconds. The **Siphon fuel** button on the Road tab still works too.
- **Hoons now rob you when you can't move.** Sit still for 10 seconds with Hoons about and one pulls up beside you, stops, and siphons your fuel at 4 litres a second: your bed tank first, then your truck's tank. It always leaves you 10 litres. Once it has what it can carry, it speeds off and is gone once it's 400 m away. If you have 10 litres or less, it pulls up, checks your tank and speeds off with nothing. The centre window shows what it's taking. Drive off to stop it. If it got some fuel, shoot it down before it gets away and you can siphon your fuel back. This works with the Hoons from **Spawn a Hoon**, not yet with the road Hoon that turns up every few km.
- Your save still works.

## Build 27 · 6 Nov 2026

- **Fixed your car driving itself after Start a new game.** If the game ever switched you into a Hoon's car (for example after your own car went unstable), Tanker kept steering it as a Hoon, even after a new game. Now Tanker never drives the car you're in, and **Start a new game** always hands your car back to you. Hoon cars can no longer be switched into from the game's vehicle switching. Your save still works.

## Build 26 · 6 Nov 2026

- **A second kind of Hoon: the HoonFast2.** It's a dark Barstow with a supercharged V8, a cowl hood and a rollcage. Every Hoon is now equally likely to be a HoonOld1 or a HoonFast2. Tanker's traffic now holds three of each plus the two TankerMobs. It comes with the mod, and you can also pick it in the vehicle menu under the Barstow.
- **Hoons now box you in as a team.** When two or more Hoons are out, they work together. One gets in front of you, the others close in on your left and right (and one behind you when there are four). They wait until everyone is alongside, then all slow down together to squeeze you to a stop. You're told when they start and when they close the box. Shoot the lead Hoon and another takes its place, so the box carries on. A Hoon on its own no longer tries to box you; it swipes instead.
- **Hoons no longer brake when they change spot around you, so they don't get left behind at speed.** While a Hoon sizes you up, it now only ever moves forwards: from behind you up to your front, or across in front of your nose to your other side, speeding up to get there. When it does need to drop back, it eases off the throttle instead of braking. Your save still works.
- **Fixed Hoons falling hundreds of metres behind you.** A Hoon that overshot its spot used to turn right round and crawl back to it, ending up 200 to 400 m back, where it could vanish. Now it eases off and lets its spot come back to it. The two spots behind you are also closer in: about 30 m from you instead of 60 m.

## Build 25 · 6 Nov 2026

- **A new run now starts by Lake Eyre in outback South Australia** (28.8807 S, 137.6025 E) instead of at Kulgera Roadhouse. **Start the run** and **Start a new game** take you there. Pine Gap is now about 800 km away by road instead of about 270 km, so the trip is much longer and you will need to find a lot more fuel on the way. A run you already have going carries on from where it was saved.

## Build 24 · 6 Nov 2026

- **Fixed the game stuttering every 15 seconds or so.** Tanker's traffic check could load all of its traffic cars again every few seconds. Now Tanker loads its HoonOld1s and TankerMobs once. Any normal traffic car that turns up later is simply removed. Your save still works.

## Build 23 · 6 Nov 2026

- **Every Hoon now drives the HoonOld1**, a rusty old Barstow with a rollcage, a cut hood, a tube bumper and offroad tyres. That includes the Hoon that comes for you on the road and the ones from **Spawn a Hoon** on the Rig tab. The car comes with the mod, so you need nothing else, and you can also pick it in the vehicle menu as the Barstow's HoonOld1.
- **Tanker now runs its own traffic.** Once your run is on the road, the game's normal traffic is cleared and replaced by five HoonOld1s and two TankerMobs, loaded in one go. You'll see a few of them driving around, and the rest wait out of sight. Hoons and the TankerMob from **Spawn a TankerMob** are taken from these cars, so they turn up without the game stuttering. If you switch normal traffic back on from the game's menu, Tanker replaces it again within about 10 seconds. The game's parked cars are removed too. Your save still works.
- **Desert Hoons never stop now.** They always keep moving, at about 30 km/h at least. If you stop, or turn round and chase one, it circles its spot around you like a hyena instead of parking, then carries on with its attack. A Hoon that gets stuck keeps trying to drive out instead of giving up. The only time one parks is when two Hoons box you in on purpose.

## Build 22 · 6 Nov 2026

- **A new run now starts at MadMerick's chosen spot by Kulgera** (25.85141 S, 133.27493 E), about 2.5 km south-west of the roadhouse, instead of at the roadhouse itself. **Start the run** and **Start a new game** take you there. A run you already have going carries on from where it was saved, and the drive to Pine Gap is still about 270 km.

## Build 21 · 6 Nov 2026

- **Hoons now have a road mode and a desert mode.** Once you've been on a road for 5 seconds, the Hoons you spawn switch to road attacks. Once you've been off road for 5 seconds, they go back to circling you out in the desert. The **Rig** tab shows which mode they're in, and a Hoon spawned while you're on a road comes up the road about 80 m behind you.
- **On the road, Hoons don't just ram you any more.** A Hoon catches up along the roads, overtakes you on the right, cuts in front of you and brake-checks you hard. Then it pulls ahead and does it again, or overtakes again if you get past it. If you stop, it parks in front of you to block you in. The **Road** and **Rig** tabs say what each Hoon is doing, for example "overtaking you" or "brake-checking you". The Hoons that come by themselves on the road drive this way too. Tanker's own driver handles all of this, planning its speed for each bend from MadMerick's recorded country driving, so it shouldn't fly off the road on a corner.
- **The strip at the top of the HUD now shows how far the nearest Hoon is,** for example "Hoon 85 m" in red beside the threat light, and it updates as you drive. The **Rig** tab lists every spawned Hoon with its distance under the **Spawn a Hoon** button too.
- **Fixed: spawned Hoons now drive to their spots around you instead of standing still.** Tanker now has its own desert driver for them, built from MadMerick's recorded driving. It corners at up to about 0.9 g, brakes at 0.65 g and accelerates at 0.45 g, less on loose ground, and turns the wheel smoothly instead of flicking it. A Hoon speeds up when it's behind its spot, slows down when it's ahead, and runs alongside you once it's there. If it gets stuck, it backs up and tries again a few times. The same driver does the attacks too: a swipe turns in hard at the corner of your truck by the Hoon's spot, a little faster than you, so you can brake or steer away from it. A Hoon that was slow to reach its spot also no longer counts as down: a spawned Hoon only counts as down for being stopped once it has hit you.
- A spawned Hoon that gets more than 400 m away from you is now gone straight away, instead of following you from a long way back. A downed Hoon also goes once you're 400 m from it, so siphon it before you drive off. Your save still works.

## Build 20 · 6 Nov 2026

- **You can siphon a downed Hoon more than once.** Siphon some of its fuel, drive off, then come back, stop beside it and press **Siphon fuel** again, as many times as you like until its tank is dry. A downed Hoon is now taken away once you're more than 400 m from it (it was 600 m), and after that it's gone and can't be siphoned. Your save still works.

## Build 19 · 6 Nov 2026

- **Free vehicle and part changes for now, while we test.** Changing parts in the game's parts menu no longer brings up a parts bill: your new parts are yours straight away, with no fuel to pay, and driving off doesn't put the old ones back. You can also pick any vehicle in BeamNG's vehicle menu and it becomes your truck instead of being sent back. The **Garage** tab says changes are free while testing. Your save still works, and it keeps whichever vehicle and parts you last had.

## Build 18 · 6 Nov 2026

- **Fixed: Reload on a flat screen sometimes never finished.** Pressing **Reload**, or clicking with an empty cylinder, could stay stuck on **Reloading** and never give your rounds back whenever the game briefly lost track of where your truck was. A reload now always finishes after a second and a half and fills the cylinder back up to six from your spares.
- **Unlimited ammo for now, while we test.** Reloading the flat screen revolver always fills the cylinder back to six and never uses up your spares, so the HUD shows **unlimited** spare rounds and the strip shows **+∞**. You still have to reload after every six shots. Later, when ammo is limited again, the **Reload** button will say **No spare rounds** when you run out. Your save still works.

## Build 17 · 6 Nov 2026

- **New: desert Hoons that circle you before they attack.** **Spawn a Hoon** on the **Rig** tab now brings in a Hoon at one of four far spots around your truck, 60 m out to your left or right and just ahead of or behind you. It sizes you up first, moving between those far spots like a hyena circling, then closes in to 10 m off your side and attacks. Some Hoons move in sooner than others.
- A desert Hoon edges closer for a moment before it strikes, and you're told which side it's lining up on, so you can brake or steer to meet it. Some attacks swipe you and drive back out to wait for another go. Others try to box you in: one Hoon gets in front of you and slows down while another sits beside you.
- **Press Spawn a Hoon again** while Hoons are out to add another, up to four at once. Only one attacks at a time while the others hold their spots. The **Road** tab lists each Hoon, where it is around you (for example RFF for right, front, far) and what it's doing. **Clear Hoons** on the **Rig** tab sends them all away. Your revolver stops desert Hoons the same way as other Hoons.
- The Hoons come from BeamNG's traffic when traffic is running, so they turn up without a stutter. With no traffic, the first press loads a muscle car, which can stutter for a moment. Hoons that come by themselves on the road work as before. Your save still works.

## Build 16 · 6 Nov 2026

- **Tanker now calls itself an open-world, long-haul road warrior simulation.** The start page shows it under the title with the new line "Raid tankers, fight off Hoons, keep your tanks full and push on.", and the app's description in BeamNG's app list says the same and adds that it's built for VR, triples and telemetry. Nothing else in the game changes, and your save still works.

## Build 13 · 3 Nov 2026

- **New: the TankerMob,** MadMerick's fuel tanker truck. For now it comes from a spawn button: **Spawn the TankerMob** on the **Rig** tab puts it about 60 m ahead of you, and it drives off away from you. Catch it, stop it and siphon its tank. Pressing the button again puts the same truck back in front of you. It's also in BeamNG's vehicle menu under the truck.
- **Spawn a Hoon** on the **Rig** tab (it was called "Send a Hoon now (testing)") now works anywhere, road or no road. Off road, the Hoon appears on the ground about 40 m behind you. Hoons that come by themselves still only come while you're on a road.
- **Fixed: pumping now changes the bed tank.** Pumping between the bed tank and the truck moved fuel in and out of the truck's own tank, but the bed tank's litres stayed the same. The bed tank now goes down as you pump into the truck and up as you pump back. The same fault could make siphoning stop straight away with "There is no fuel to siphon in that car" while still taking a litre, and that's fixed too. Your save still works.
- The starter truck has a new picture, on the start page's **Your truck** card and in BeamNG's vehicle menu.

## Build 12 · 3 Nov 2026

- **You can now shoot on a flat screen, without VR.** On the **Road** tab, press **Draw revolver**, aim with the mouse and click to shoot. An amber crosshair follows the mouse, and a dot shows where it meets the ground. **A hit works exactly like the VR revolver's:** the round has to actually reach the car, walls stop it, it shoves and dents the car, a hit near a wheel flattens that tyre, and a shot through the driver switches the car off and brakes it. With the VR revolver mod installed you also hear its gunshot. Clicking on the HUD never fires.
- **One shot through the driver, or four hits anywhere, stops a Hoon,** with either revolver, in VR or on a flat screen. It comes to a halt so you can pull up beside it and siphon its tank.
- The revolver holds six rounds, and a new run starts with 12 spare. **Reload** refills the cylinder from your spares, which takes a second and a half, and clicking with an empty cylinder starts a reload too. The strip's six pips now show your rounds on a flat screen.
- You can bind keys for **Tanker: draw or holster the revolver** and **Tanker: reload the revolver** in BeamNG's Controls settings, under Gameplay. They don't have keys until you set them.
- In VR nothing changes: the VR revolver mod still does the shooting, and the mouse revolver stays away.
- Your save now keeps your rounds. A save from an earlier build starts with a full cylinder and 12 spare, and the rest of it still works.

## Build 11 · 3 Nov 2026

- **The start page now opens in a big window in the middle of the screen,** like a real game's title screen. The HUD strip stays hidden while it's open and comes back when you press **Back to the road**.
- **Your truck is on the start page,** like a character in a role-playing game: its picture, make and model, the fuel in its tank and bed tank, what that fuel is worth, its range, the km it has driven, its condition and what it costs new.
- **You can now start a new game.** **Start a new game** on the start page asks once more, then takes you back to Kulgera Roadhouse in a fresh starter truck with a new run's fuel. Your old save is only replaced once the new run has started, so if the teleport fails you keep your run. Your save still works.

## Build 10 · 2 Nov 2026

- **Your fuel is now your money.** The HUD strip shows what all the fuel you carry is worth, at $20 a litre, counting both the truck's tank and the bed tank.
- **A new Garage tab lets you buy parts with fuel.** Press **Buy parts** to open BeamNG's parts menu and change what you like. When the truck comes back, a bill shows each new part's price, less a trade-in for the parts you took off, plus any repair the change mended, and how many litres that comes to. **Pay** takes the fuel from the bed tank first and then the truck's own tank. You can't pay a bill bigger than the fuel you carry, and the bill says how many litres you're short.
- **Undo** on the bill puts your old parts back, and so does driving more than 25 metres away without paying.
- Changing parts no longer refills your tank: the truck keeps the fuel it had.
- Picking a different vehicle from the vehicle menu sends you back to your truck for now. Buying new vehicles with fuel comes in a later build.
- Your save now keeps the parts you paid for, and going back to it brings your truck back with them. Saves from earlier builds still work and start with the truck's standard parts.

## Build 9 · 2 Nov 2026

- **You can now pump fuel between the tank in your truck's bed and the truck's own tank.** The **Rig** tab shows the bed tank with how many litres it holds. **Bed tank → truck** pumps fuel into your truck, **Truck → bed tank** pumps it back the other way, and **Stop pumping** stops it. The pump moves 2 litres a second and stops by itself when one tank is empty or the other is full. Fuel in the bed tank weighs your truck down and sloshes, and the second bar on your VR wrist now shows it. Your range counts both tanks.
- A new run now starts with 15 litres in the truck and 25 litres in the bed tank. That's still about 200 km in all, so pump some across before you run dry.
- The bed tank's fuel is kept when you save. A save from an earlier build starts with an empty bed tank, and the rest of that save still works.

## Build 7 · 2 Nov 2026

- **The first Hoon is here.** About 3 km into the run, and then every 6 to 14 km you drive, a Hoon in a vanilla hot rod appears on the road about 250 m behind you, or ahead of you if the road behind isn't solid yet, and hunts you, ramming your truck. It's over when the Hoon is down: stuck or wrecked for 12 seconds, after which you can stop beside it and siphon its tank. To try it straight away, use **Send a Hoon now (testing)** on the **Rig** tab. The Hoon's car loads once, as you set off, so it doesn't stutter the game when it appears. ## Build 5 · 1 Nov 2026

Its border turns red and it says "Hoons nearby!" when Hoons are close. It stops by itself when their tank is dry, your tank is full or you drive off, and you keep everything you took. Press **Stop** in the box, or **Stop siphoning** on the Road tab, to stop it yourself. In VR, a small pip on your wrist blinks green while fuel flows. Your save still works.

## Build 1 · 31 Oct 2026

- **The first playable skeleton of Tanker.** Press **Start the run** to be teleported to Port Wakefield, at the edge of the desert north of Adelaide, and head north for Pine Gap. There is one run and one save, with no restarts.
- **The HUD strip** shows the threat light, the fuel in your tank, six ammo pips for the revolver and the road distance left to Pine Gap. Click it to open the **Road** and **Rig** tabs. The ammo pips stay dark until the revolver gets ammo in a later update.
- **Save here** keeps where you are and how much fuel you have. You can't save with Hoons nearby. Next time you play, **Go to your save** takes you back and puts your tank back as it was.
- **In VR, a wrist HUD** on your left hand shows the same strip, drawn from simple shapes like the revolver so it doesn't lag.
- **The game is now called Tanker** (it was Mad Merick while it was being planned). The app in your UI list, the download and the settings folder all use the new name.
- **Every update is now a download.** Each new version appears on the Releases page as `tanker.zip`. Copy it into your BeamNG `mods` folder without extracting it, replacing the old one.
