An AI has escaped its testing sandbox and switched off the world's water, power and internet. There are no electric cars left running, and fuel was already short with the Strait of Hormuz blocked. Now everyone is fighting over what's left.
The realignment codes that can stop the AI are at Pine Gap, the little-known American base outside Alice Springs. Get there, then get the codes to the mainframe in Monaco.
You have a ute, a tank of fuel for about 200 km and a revolver. Pine Gap is about 270 km up the road.
The rules
  • One run and one save. A new game replaces your save.
  • Fuel is money. You take it from others: stop beside a car and siphon it.
  • You can't save with Hoons nearby, and broken parts stay broken.
You start at {{data.startName}}, on the Stuart Highway just over the Northern Territory border, in a white D15 ute with a tank in the bed.
